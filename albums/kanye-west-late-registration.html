About Late Registration

“Late Registration” is a 2005 release by Kanye West. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Wake Up Mr. West” and ending with “Diamonds From Sierra Leone”.

In our collection of 16 albums by Kanye West, it comes after “The College Dropout” (2004) and before “Graduation” (2007).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 15 tracks across Kanye West's albums here.

The tracklist includes 4 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Skit #1”, “Skit #2”, “Skit #3”, “Skit #4”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
