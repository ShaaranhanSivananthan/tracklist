About Room for Improvement

“Room for Improvement” is a 2006 release by Drake. On the version used in Order the Album it has 23 tracks, starting with “Intro” and ending with “U.P.A. (Outro)”.

In our collection of 20 albums by Drake, it is the earliest, followed by “Comeback Season” (2007).

With 23 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Drake's albums here.
