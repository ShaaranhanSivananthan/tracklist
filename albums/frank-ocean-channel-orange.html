About Channel Orange

Channel Orange (2012) was Frank Ocean's debut studio album, following the mixtape Nostalgia, Ultra. Its 17 tracks begin with "Start" and end with "End," which gives you two easy anchors. In between are songs like "Thinkin Bout You," "Sweet Life," "Super Rich Kids" and the long, multi-part "Pyramids." André 3000 appears on "Pink Matter." The album won the Grammy for Best Urban Contemporary Album and is a good example of how soul and R&B can tell stories across a full record.
