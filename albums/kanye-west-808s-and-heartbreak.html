About 808s & Heartbreak

808s & Heartbreak (2008) is a short, stark album of 12 tracks, built on the Roland TR-808 drum machine that gives it its name. Kanye West sings through Auto-Tune instead of rapping, and the songs deal with loss and isolation. It opens with "Say You Will" and includes "Heartless," "Love Lockdown" and "Paranoid," and it ends with "Pinocchio Story." The record had a large influence on the pop and hip-hop that followed, and its sparse sound makes each track easy to tell apart.

“808s & Heartbreak” is a 2008 release by Kanye West. On the version used in Order the Album it has 12 tracks, starting with “Say You Will” and ending with “Pinocchio Story”.
