About Infinite

“Infinite” is a 1996 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 11 tracks, starting with “Infinite” and ending with “Jealousy Woes II”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it is the earliest, followed by “The Slim Shady LP” (1999).

With 11 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 1 intro, interlude or skit-style track: “W.E.G.O. (Interlude)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
