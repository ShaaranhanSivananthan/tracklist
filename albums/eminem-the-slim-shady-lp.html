About The Slim Shady LP

The Slim Shady LP (1999) was Eminem's major-label debut, released through Dr. Dre's Aftermath label. It has 20 tracks, including five skits, and features "My Name Is," "Guilty Conscience" and "Role Model." A quarter of the album is made up of short spoken pieces, which makes it one of the harder tracklists to learn, since skits are easy to forget and easy to confuse. The best strategy is to learn the songs first and then slot the skits in between them.

“The Slim Shady LP” is a 1999 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Public Service Announcement” and ending with “Still Don't Give a F***”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “Infinite” (1996) and before “The Marshall Mathers LP” (2000).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 5 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Paul (Skit)”, “B**** (Skit)”, “Lounge (Skit)”, “Ken Kaniff (Skit)”, “Soap (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
