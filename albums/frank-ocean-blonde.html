About Blonde

“Blonde” is a 2016 release by Frank Ocean. On the version used in Order the Album it has 17 tracks, starting with “Nikes” and ending with “Futura Free”.

In our collection of 4 albums by Frank Ocean, it is the most recent, following “Endless” (2016).

With 17 tracks, it is exactly the average of 17 tracks across Frank Ocean's albums here.
