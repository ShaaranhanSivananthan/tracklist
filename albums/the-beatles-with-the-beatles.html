About With the Beatles

“With the Beatles” is a 1963 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “It Won't Be Long” and ending with “Money (That's What I Want)”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Please Please Me” (1963) and before “A Hard Day's Night” (1964).

With 14 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
