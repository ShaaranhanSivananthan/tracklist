About Thank U, Next

Thank U, Next (2019) has 12 tracks and was released in February 2019, less than six months after Sweetener. It opens with "Imagine" and includes "7 Rings," "Bad Idea," "Needy" and the title track, and it ends with "Break Up with Your Girlfriend, I'm Bored." The title track and the closer are both singles, which gives you two strong anchors late in the order, so the middle tracks are where you should focus your practice.
