About When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?

Billie Eilish's debut album (2019) has 14 tracks and was written and recorded with her brother FINNEAS, largely at home. It opens with a short, odd track titled with a string of exclamation marks, includes "Bad Guy," "Bury a Friend" and "When the Party's Over," and ends with "Goodbye," which briefly revisits fragments of earlier songs. It won the Grammy for Album of the Year in 2020. The bookends make it memorable, but the middle tracks share a similar mood, so they take more practice.
