About Beatles for Sale

“Beatles for Sale” is a 1964 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “No Reply” and ending with “Everybody's Trying to Be My Baby”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “A Hard Day's Night” (1964) and before “Help!” (1965).

With 14 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
