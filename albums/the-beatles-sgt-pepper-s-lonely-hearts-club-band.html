About Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band

Released in 1967, Sgt. Pepper presents itself as a concert by a fictional band. It opens with the title track, hands the microphone to a character called Billy Shears for "With a Little Help from My Friends," and brings the title song back as a reprise at track 12. The album then ends with "A Day in the Life," which sits outside the concert frame. With 13 tracks and short gaps between songs, it is often cited as the point where the album became a designed work rather than a collection of singles.

“Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band” is a 1967 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 13 tracks, starting with “Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band” and ending with “A Day in the Life”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Revolver” (1966) and before “Magical Mystery Tour” (1967).
