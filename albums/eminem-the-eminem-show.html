About The Eminem Show

“The Eminem Show” is a 2002 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Curtains Up (Skit)” and ending with “Curtains Close (Skit)”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “The Marshall Mathers LP” (2000) and before “Encore” (2004).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 5 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Curtains Up (Skit)”, “The Kiss (Skit)”, “Paul Rosenberg (Skit)”, “Steve Berman (Skit)”, “Curtains Close (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
