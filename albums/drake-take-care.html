About Take Care

Take Care (2011) has 18 tracks and a moody, atmospheric sound, with production largely by Noah "40" Shebib. It opens with "Over My Dead Body" and includes "Headlines," "Marvins Room," the Rihanna duet "Take Care," "Crew Love" with The Weeknd, and "Buried Alive Interlude" with Kendrick Lamar. It won the Grammy for Best Rap Album and remains one of the albums people cite most when they talk about Drake's sound. Two interlude-style tracks make it a little trickier than a plain list of songs.

“Take Care” is a 2011 release by Drake. On the version used in Order the Album it has 18 tracks, starting with “Over My Dead Body” and ending with “The Ride”.

In our collection of 20 albums by Drake, it comes after “Thank Me Later” (2010) and before “Nothing Was the Same” (2013).

With 18 tracks, it is exactly the average of 18 tracks across Drake's albums here.

The tracklist includes 2 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Buried Alive Interlude”, “Cameras / Good Ones Go Interlude”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
