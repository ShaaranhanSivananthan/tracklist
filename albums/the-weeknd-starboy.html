About Starboy

“Starboy” is a 2016 release by The Weeknd. On the version used in Order the Album it has 18 tracks, starting with “Starboy” and ending with “I Feel It Coming”.

In our collection of 10 albums by The Weeknd, it comes after “Beauty Behind The Madness” (2015) and before “My Dear Melancholy,” (2018).

With 18 tracks, it is longer than the average of 13 tracks across The Weeknd's albums here.
