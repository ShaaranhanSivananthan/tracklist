About The Off-Season

“The Off-Season” is a 2021 release by J. Cole. On the version used in Order the Album it has 12 tracks, starting with “95 South” and ending with “Hunger on Hillside”.

In our collection of 11 albums by J. Cole, it comes after “KOD” (2018) and before “Might Delete Later” (2024).

With 12 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 16 tracks across J. Cole's albums here.
