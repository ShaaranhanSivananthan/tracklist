About Dark & Wild

“Dark & Wild” is a 2014 release by BTS. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “Intro: What Am I To You” and ending with “Outro: Do You Think It Makes Sense?”.

In our collection of 6 albums by BTS, it is the earliest, followed by “Wings” (2016).

With 14 tracks, it is exactly the average of 14 tracks across BTS's albums here.

The tracklist includes 3 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Intro: What Am I To You”, “Interlude: What Are You Doing Now”, “Outro: Do You Think It Makes Sense?”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
