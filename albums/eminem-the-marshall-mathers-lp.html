About The Marshall Mathers LP

The Marshall Mathers LP (2000) followed the success of The Slim Shady LP and became one of the fastest-selling albums in US history at the time. It has 18 tracks, three of them skits, and includes "Stan," which is built around a sample of Dido's "Thank You," along with "The Real Slim Shady" and "The Way I Am." It opens with "Public Service Announcement 2000" and ends with "Criminal." The skits recur from album to album, so check the titles carefully when you place them.

“The Marshall Mathers LP” is a 2000 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 18 tracks, starting with “Public Service Announcement 2000” and ending with “Criminal”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “The Slim Shady LP” (1999) and before “The Eminem Show” (2002).

With 18 tracks, it is exactly the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 3 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Paul (Skit)”, “Steve Berman (Skit)”, “Ken Kaniff (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
