About Views

“Views” is a 2016 release by Drake. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Keep the Family Close” and ending with “Hotline Bling”.

In our collection of 20 albums by Drake, it comes after “What a Time to Be Alive” (2015) and before “More Life” (2017).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Drake's albums here.

The tracklist includes 1 intro, interlude or skit-style track: “Summers Over Interlude”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
