About To Pimp a Butterfly

“To Pimp a Butterfly” is a 2015 release by Kendrick Lamar. On the version used in Order the Album it has 16 tracks, starting with “Wesley's Theory” and ending with “Mortal Man”.

In our collection of 6 albums by Kendrick Lamar, it comes after “good kid, m.A.A.d city” (2012) and before “Damn” (2017).

With 16 tracks, it is longer than the average of 15 tracks across Kendrick Lamar's albums here.

The tracklist includes 2 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “For Free? (Interlude)”, “For Sale? (Interlude)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
