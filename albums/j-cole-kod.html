About KOD

“KOD” is a 2018 release by J. Cole. On the version used in Order the Album it has 12 tracks, starting with “Intro” and ending with “1985 (Intro to The Fall Off)”.

In our collection of 11 albums by J. Cole, it comes after “4 Your Eyez Only” (2016) and before “The Off-Season” (2021).

With 12 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 16 tracks across J. Cole's albums here.

The tracklist includes 4 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Intro”, “Once an Addict (Interlude)”, “Window Pain (Outro)”, “1985 (Intro to The Fall Off)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
