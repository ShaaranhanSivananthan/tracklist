About Endless

“Endless” is a 2016 release by Frank Ocean. On the version used in Order the Album it has 21 tracks, starting with “Device Control” and ending with “Device Control (reprise)”.

In our collection of 4 albums by Frank Ocean, it comes after “Channel Orange” (2012) and before “Blonde” (2016).
