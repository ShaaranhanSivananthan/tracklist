About Born Sinner

“Born Sinner” is a 2013 release by J. Cole. On the version used in Order the Album it has 16 tracks, starting with “Villuminati” and ending with “Born Sinner”.

In our collection of 11 albums by J. Cole, it comes after “The Sideline Story” (2011) and before “2014 Forest Hills Drive” (2014).

With 16 tracks, it is exactly the average of 16 tracks across J. Cole's albums here.

The tracklist includes 4 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Kerney Sermon (Skit)”, “Mo Money (Interlude)”, “Where's Jermaine? (Skit)”, “Ain't That Some S*** (Interlude)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
