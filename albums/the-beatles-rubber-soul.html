About Rubber Soul

“Rubber Soul” is a 1965 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “Drive My Car” and ending with “Run for Your Life”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Help!” (1965) and before “Revolver” (1966).

With 14 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
