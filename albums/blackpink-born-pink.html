About Born Pink

Born Pink (2022) is BLACKPINK's second full-length album and has 8 tracks. It opens with "Pink Venom," followed by "Shut Down," and includes "Typa Girl," "Yeah Yeah Yeah," "Hard to Love," "The Happiest Girl," "Tally" and "Ready for Love." With only eight tracks and two big singles near the front, it is a quick album to learn and a good one for beginners. With so few tracks, even a small mistake shows up right away.
