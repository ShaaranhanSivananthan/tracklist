About Revolver

Revolver (1966) has 14 tracks and changes style constantly. It opens with George Harrison's "Taxman," includes the string-quartet arrangement of "Eleanor Rigby" and the sing-along "Yellow Submarine," and ends with "Tomorrow Never Knows," built on tape loops and studio effects. It is useful for training your ear because almost every track sounds different from its neighbors, which gives each position its own identity. On the original vinyl, side one ends with "She Said She Said" and side two begins with "Good Day Sunshine," a handy split into two sets of seven.

“Revolver” is a 1966 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “Taxman” and ending with “Tomorrow Never Knows”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Rubber Soul” (1965) and before “Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band” (1967).
