About After Hours

“After Hours” is a 2020 release by The Weeknd. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “Alone Again” and ending with “Until I Bleed Out”.

In our collection of 10 albums by The Weeknd, it comes after “My Dear Melancholy,” (2018) and before “Dawn FM” (2022).
