About Wings

“Wings” is a 2016 release by BTS. On the version used in Order the Album it has 15 tracks, starting with “Intro: Boy Meets Evil” and ending with “Interlude: Wings”.

In our collection of 6 albums by BTS, it comes after “Dark & Wild” (2014) and before “Love Yourself: Tear” (2018).

With 15 tracks, it is longer than the average of 14 tracks across BTS's albums here.

The tracklist includes 2 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Intro: Boy Meets Evil”, “Interlude: Wings”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
