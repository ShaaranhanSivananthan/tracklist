About Graduation

Graduation (2007) has 13 tracks and trades the soul samples of Kanye West's earlier work for big, stadium-sized synths. It opens with "Good Morning" and ends with "Big Brother," and in between are "Stronger," which samples Daft Punk, "Can't Tell Me Nothing," "Flashing Lights" and "Homecoming." It was released on the same day as 50 Cent's Curtis, which turned into a much-discussed sales contest. With no skits and a steady pace, it is a clean album to practice on.

“Graduation” is a 2007 release by Kanye West. On the version used in Order the Album it has 13 tracks, starting with “Good Morning” and ending with “Big Brother”.
