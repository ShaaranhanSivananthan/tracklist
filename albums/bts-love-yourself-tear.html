About Love Yourself: Tear

Love Yourself: Tear (2018) has 11 tracks and became the first K-pop album to reach number one on the Billboard 200. It opens with "Intro: Singularity" and ends with "Outro: Tear," giving it a clear frame, and includes "Fake Love," "Paradise," "Magic Shop" and "Anpanman." The group moves between rap and singing, and between dance-pop and moodier tracks. The intro and outro make it easy to place the ends, so the challenge is the nine songs in between.
