About The College Dropout

The College Dropout (2004) was Kanye West's debut and has 21 tracks, including several skits that give it the feel of a school day. It features "Jesus Walks," "All Falls Down," "Slow Jamz" and "Through the Wire," the last of which he recorded while recovering from a jaw injury after a car accident. The album ends with "Last Call," a track of around twelve minutes in which he tells the story of his path to this record. With skits and a long closer, it has plenty of landmarks to hold on to.

“The College Dropout” is a 2004 release by Kanye West. On the version used in Order the Album it has 21 tracks, starting with “Intro” and ending with “Last Call”.

In our collection of 16 albums by Kanye West, it is the earliest, followed by “Late Registration” (2005).

With 21 tracks, it is longer than the average of 15 tracks across Kanye West's albums here.

The tracklist includes 4 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Intro”, “School Spirit Skit 1”, “School Spirit Skit 2”, “Lil Jimmy Skit”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
