About Help!

“Help!” is a 1965 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “Help!” and ending with “Dizzy Miss Lizzy”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Beatles for Sale” (1964) and before “Rubber Soul” (1965).

With 14 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
