About A Hard Day's Night

“A Hard Day's Night” is a 1964 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 13 tracks, starting with “A Hard Day's Night” and ending with “I'll Be Back”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “With the Beatles” (1963) and before “Beatles for Sale” (1964).

With 13 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
