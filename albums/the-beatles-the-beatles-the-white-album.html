About The Beatles (The White Album)

The 1968 double album known as the White Album has 30 tracks and a plain white cover. It is the Beatles at their most scattered and their most free, moving from the rock of "Back in the U.S.S.R." and "Helter Skelter" to the acoustic "Blackbird," the sound collage "Revolution 9," and the lullaby "Good Night" that closes the set. Because it is so varied, it is one of the hardest Beatles albums to rebuild from memory, and one of the easiest to break into smaller chunks by mood.

“The Beatles (The White Album)” is a 1968 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 30 tracks, starting with “Back in the U.S.S.R.” and ending with “Good Night”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Magical Mystery Tour” (1967) and before “Yellow Submarine” (1969).
