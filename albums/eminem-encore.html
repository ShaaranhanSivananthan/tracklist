About Encore

“Encore” is a 2004 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Curtains Up” and ending with “Encore / Curtains Down”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “The Eminem Show” (2002) and before “Relapse” (2009).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 3 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Paul (Skit)”, “Em Calls Paul (Skit)”, “Final Thought (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
