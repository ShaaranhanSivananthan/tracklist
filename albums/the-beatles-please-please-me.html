About Please Please Me

“Please Please Me” is a 1963 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 14 tracks, starting with “I Saw Her Standing There” and ending with “Twist and Shout”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it is the earliest, followed by “With the Beatles” (1963).

With 14 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
