About Kamikaze

“Kamikaze” is a 2018 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 13 tracks, starting with “The Ringer” and ending with “Venom”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “Revival” (2017) and before “Music to Be Murdered By” (2020).

With 13 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 2 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Paul (Skit)”, “Em Calls Paul (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
