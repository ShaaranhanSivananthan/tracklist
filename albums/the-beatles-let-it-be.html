About Let It Be

“Let It Be” is a 1970 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 12 tracks, starting with “Two of Us” and ending with “Get Back”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it is the most recent, following “Abbey Road” (1969).

With 12 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
