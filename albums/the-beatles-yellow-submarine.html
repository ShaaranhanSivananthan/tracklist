About Yellow Submarine

“Yellow Submarine” is a 1969 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 13 tracks, starting with “Yellow Submarine” and ending with “Yellow Submarine in Pepperland”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “The Beatles (The White Album)” (1968) and before “Abbey Road” (1969).

With 13 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
