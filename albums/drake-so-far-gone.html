About So Far Gone

“So Far Gone” is a 2009 release by Drake. On the version used in Order the Album it has 17 tracks, starting with “Lust for Life” and ending with “Brand New”.

In our collection of 20 albums by Drake, it comes after “Comeback Season” (2007) and before “Thank Me Later” (2010).

With 17 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 18 tracks across Drake's albums here.
