About Future Nostalgia

Future Nostalgia (2020) has 11 tracks and is a focused, polished dance-pop album with strong disco and 1980s influences. It opens with its title track and includes "Don't Start Now," "Physical," "Levitating" and "Break My Heart" before finishing with "Boys Will Be Boys." It was released in March 2020, a week earlier than planned. Nearly every track has a clear rhythmic identity and an early hook, so it is one of the easiest albums in the game to rebuild if you know the singles.
