About Magical Mystery Tour

“Magical Mystery Tour” is a 1967 release by The Beatles. On the version used in Order the Album it has 11 tracks, starting with “Magical Mystery Tour” and ending with “All You Need Is Love”.

In our collection of 13 albums by The Beatles, it comes after “Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band” (1967) and before “The Beatles (The White Album)” (1968).

With 11 tracks, it is shorter than the average of 15 tracks across The Beatles's albums here.
