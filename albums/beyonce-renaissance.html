About Renaissance

Renaissance (2022) is a dance album drawing on house, disco and club music, and its 16 tracks are sequenced so that many of them flow into one another like a DJ set. It opens with "I'm That Girl," includes "Break My Soul" and "Cuff It," and closes with "Summer Renaissance." It was presented as the first part of a planned trilogy, which continued with Cowboy Carter. Hearing it in order is a very different experience from hearing the singles one at a time, and it is a good album to try if you like continuous mixes.

“Renaissance” is a 2022 release by Beyoncé. On the version used in Order the Album it has 16 tracks, starting with “I'm That Girl” and ending with “Summer Renaissance”.
