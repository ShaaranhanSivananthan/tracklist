About 2014 Forest Hills Drive

2014 Forest Hills Drive (2014) is named after the address of the house in Fayetteville, North Carolina where J. Cole grew up, and its 13 tracks follow a loose story of growing up and coming home. It was widely noted for having no guest appearances. It begins with "Intro" and includes "Wet Dreamz," "No Role Modelz," "Love Yourz" and "Apparently," before ending with "Note to Self." Because it plays like a story, the order is easier to follow than most hip-hop albums.
