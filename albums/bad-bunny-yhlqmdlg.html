About YHLQMDLG

YHLQMDLG, short for "Yo Hago Lo Que Me Da La Gana," was released on February 29, 2020 and has 20 tracks. It is a love letter to classic reggaeton and includes "Si Veo a Tu Mamá," "Yo Perreo Sola" and "Safaera," a track famous for shifting through several distinct sections. The album opens with "Si Veo a Tu Mamá" and ends with "<3." Its high energy and varied titles give most tracks a clear identity, which helps when you are rebuilding the order.
