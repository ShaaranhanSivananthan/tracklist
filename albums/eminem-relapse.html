About Relapse

“Relapse” is a 2009 release by Eminem. On the version used in Order the Album it has 20 tracks, starting with “Dr. West (Skit)” and ending with “Underground”.

In our collection of 12 albums by Eminem, it comes after “Encore” (2004) and before “Recovery” (2010).

With 20 tracks, it is longer than the average of 18 tracks across Eminem's albums here.

The tracklist includes 5 intro, interlude or skit-style tracks: “Dr. West (Skit)”, “Tonya (Skit)”, “Paul (Skit)”, “Mr. Mathers (Skit)”, “Steve Berman (Skit)”. Short tracks like these are easy to forget, which makes the order harder to remember.
