Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

At Order the Album, accessible from orderthealbum.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Order the Album and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Order the Album is designed to be played directly in your browser without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless voluntarily provided through direct contact (such as support emails).

2. Log Files

Like many standard websites, Order the Album follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to orderthealbum.com and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Order the Album's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

5. Children's Information

Order the Album does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
