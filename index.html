What Is Order the Album?

Order the Album is a music trivia game that tests how well you know your favorite albums. Choose an artist, select an album, and arrange its tracks in the original album order.

Instead of answering questions about an artist or identifying a song from a short clip, Order the Album focuses on something every album listener knows: the order of the tracks.

How to Play

  1. Choose an artist. Select an artist from the collection above.
  2. Choose an album. Pick the album you want to play.
  3. Arrange the tracks. Drag the tracks into the order they appear on the album.
  4. Check your answer. Submit your tracklist to see how many positions you got correct.
  5. Share your result. Download, share, or copy your result image after completing an album.

How Scoring Works

Your score is based on the number of tracks that are in their exact correct position in the album's standard tracklist.

For example, if an album has 12 tracks and 10 are in their correct positions, your score is 10 / 12, or 83%.

A track only counts as correct when it occupies the same position as it does in the album tracklist used by the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Order the Album?

Order the Album is a music trivia game where you arrange album tracks in their original order and test how well you remember an album's tracklist.

How do I play?

Choose an artist, select an album, drag the tracks into the correct order, and select Check Answer when you are finished.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is the number of tracks that are in their exact correct position, compared with the total number of tracks on the album.

Can I share my result?

Yes. After completing an album, you can download, share, or copy an image showing your result.

Do I need an account to play?

No account is required to play Order the Album.

Are deluxe editions included?

The game uses the standard album tracklists rather than deluxe or special-edition bonus tracks.

About the Project

Order the Album is an independent project built around a simple idea: knowing an album means more than recognizing individual songs. It also means remembering how those songs fit together as a complete tracklist.

The site is maintained as an ongoing project, with artists, albums, artwork, and tracklists added and updated over time.

The album collection is regularly reviewed and updated as the project grows.
