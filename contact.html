Corrections and suggestions

The most helpful reports include the artist, album title, the track or detail in question, and a reliable source showing the correct information. Please also mention whether your source refers to a standard, deluxe, regional, or reissued edition.

For now, you can contact the project maintainer through the repository's issue tracker:

Please do not include private information in a public issue. If you prefer not to make a public report, use GitHub's available contact options for the repository owner.
