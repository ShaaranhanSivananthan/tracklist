About Order the Album

Testing how well you know your favorite albums from start to finish.

Order the Album is an independent music trivia game built around a simple premise: true album fans don't just recognize individual hit singles—they remember the flow, sequencing, and tracklists of complete albums.

Why We Built It

Traditional music trivia focuses on trivia facts, release dates, or guessing songs from 2-second audio clips. We wanted to create an interactive experience that celebrates the art of album sequencing. Whether it's classic rock records, pop masterpieces, or rap landmarks, arranging tracks in their original release order challenges your music memory in a fresh way.

How It Works

Players pick their favorite artist, select an album, and drag-and-drop the shuffled tracklist back into its correct standard album order. Once submitted, the game scores your accuracy and lets you download and share your results card with friends.

Continuous Updates

We regularly expand our library with new artists, albums, and tracklists to keep the trivia fresh and engaging for music lovers around the world.
